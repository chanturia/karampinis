## Copy

Copy is rewritten for the new design, rather than reproducing old legal text.
No dated fees, lesson counts, licence validity periods, success guarantees,
“best price” claims or Ferrari promotion have been carried over.
No unverified fleet, instructors, adapted vehicles or accessibility features are claimed.
The old doctor's directory is represented by a medical-examination information page;
names, phone numbers and “contracted” status need current confirmation before publishing a directory.
The old contact page renders unresolved Joomla module placeholders; the verified school
address and main phone are used instead. No fake contact form or unverified email.

The old Word documents were fetched and their readable embedded text inspected:
replacement is the conversion to a new/community-type licence; copy covers loss/theft/damage.
They are not republished as current forms.

no quizzes, question bank, score reporting or account integration are hosted here.
